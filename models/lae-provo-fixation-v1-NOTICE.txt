LAE Provo Fixation v1 - data attribution notice

This model is derived from:

  Steven G. Luke and Kiel Christianson, "The Provo Corpus: A Large
  Eye-Tracking Corpus with Predictability Norms," Behavior Research Methods
  50 (2018), 826-833. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-017-0908-4

Modifications: LAE aggregated each word's reader-level non-skip observations
into fixation probability, retained tokens that map to one LAE English unit,
selected a ridge-linear length/function model using passage-grouped five-fold
cross-validation, refit the selected model on all compatible units, quantized
its parameters to binary32, and compiled those parameters into a .lem artifact.
No raw participant observations or passage text are included in the artifact.

derived model.
