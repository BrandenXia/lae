LAE aggregates participant observations by word and trains passage-grouped
linear predictors for (1) fixation probability and (2) normalized horizontal
first-fixation landing position. The runtime projects those predictions onto
provider-supplied lexical-core subunits or a learned partial prefix. The raw
corpus and participant-level records are not redistributed.
